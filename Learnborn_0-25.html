How v0.25 learns

World consequences are real state changes. Shots teach HIT, MISS, or BARREL from real collisions or expiry. Predictions use recorded outcomes. Movement and attacks are observations; the engine does not manufacture SUCCESS labels.

The Boot Sector is one continuous world. Viewport resizing reveals more world instead of stretching it: both projected screen axes use one uniform pixel scale. Visible barrels are solid and block movement and Debugger shots. Learnborn now chooses movement and firing on independent channels, so it can move while firing.
